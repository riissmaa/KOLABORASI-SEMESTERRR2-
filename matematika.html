PENGALAMAN MEMBUAT WEBSITE

Building a digital project from scratch has been an incredible journey. It all started in Figma, where I had to translate ideas into visual forms, organizing layouts, and choosing the right color palettes to ensure the design was not only aesthetic but also functional. The transition from design to coding was the real challenge; turning visual elements into lines of code using HTML and CSS required extra precision to achieve a pixel-perfect result.

Of course, the process wasn't always smooth. There were moments when the layout broke or features didn't function as intended. However, that's where the real learning happened—debugging, fixing the code, and ensuring everything ran perfectly. The climax was the presentation session, where I had to explain the entire process and the logic behind my work.

A huge thank you to Bu Uli and Pak Bagus for guiding me throughout this process. Thanks to their mentorship and feedback, I didn't just successfully complete this project, but I also gained a deeper understanding of what it means to be a resilient web developer.
